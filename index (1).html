About me

I studied Political Science (BSS) at Chittagong Govt Women College, and my curiosity about technology led me into programming. My programming journey started with curiosity: I wanted to know how the websites I use every day actually work. I began with HTML and CSS, then moved to JavaScript and React, and now I build full stack apps with Next.js, authentication and databases. I also study cybersecurity, so I think about safe login, data protection and common attacks from the first line of code.

I enjoy work where design and logic meet: building clean interfaces, connecting them to real data, and making them work well on every screen. Debugging a stubborn problem and finally fixing it is my favorite feeling.

Outside programming, I like to learn new things, spend time with family and friends, and explore creative hobbies. I am patient, curious and always ready to ask questions when I am stuck.
